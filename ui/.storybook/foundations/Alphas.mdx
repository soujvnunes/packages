# Alphas

Every role colour comes in six alpha steps, and each step has one job. The steps apply to backgrounds, borders and text alike.

- **Primary, 100:** text and backgrounds only, never a border.
- **Secondary, 80:** a hovered input border, and low-emphasis text on a role whose primary is 100.
- **Tertiary, 60:** an idle input border, a toned button background, lower or disabled text.
- **Quaternary, 40:** the border or background of a disabled control.
- **Quinary, 20:** faint fills and shadows.
- **Senary, 10:** faint fills and shadows.

In code a step is a token, never an opacity modifier: the primary step is the bare role (`bg-main`, `text-neutral`) and every other step carries its number (`text-neutral-60`, `border-neutral-60` with `hover:border-neutral-80`). The primary step of `neutral` on a light surface already holds 80, so its secondary step looks the same there.

## What each step is for

- **Text:** `text-neutral` for running text.
- **Low-emphasis text:** `text-neutral-60` for low-emphasis, lower or disabled text.
- **Borders:** `border-neutral-60` idle, `hover:border-neutral-80` hovered, `disabled:border-neutral-40` disabled. A border never starts at the primary step.
- **Disabled:** `disabled:border-neutral-40` and `disabled:text-neutral-60`.
- **Faint fills:** `bg-main-10` behind a selected row, `shadow-neutral-20` under a raised card.
