Add the `Button`, `Input` and `Badge` skeletons, each with its stylesheet in `@soujvnunes/ui/styles.css` and its own subpath. A `Button` or an `Input` publishes `--ui-inner-radius-40` for a `Badge` inside it, and the package now declares itself free of side effects apart from its CSS.
