Add the `Button`, `Input` and `Badge` skeletons, each with its stylesheet in `@soujvnunes/ui/styles.css` and its own subpath. Each pads inline two steps below its height: 12, 16 and 24 for a 24, 40 and 64 `Button`, with `--radius-12`, `--radius-24` and `--radius-40`, 16 and 24 for a 40 and 64 `Input`, which has no small size since a 24 high field cannot be typed in or touched, and 12 with `--radius-12` for the 24 high `Badge`. The card kind of the `Button` pads 8 by 4, 12 by 8 and 16 by 12 with `--radius-12`, `--radius-16` and `--radius-24`. Every control reads the inner radius of a field or a card around it before its own, a `Button` or an `Input` publishes `--ui-inner-radius-40`, its radius less its padding, for a `Badge` inside it. Each control is painted from one colour, `--button-color`, `--input-color` or `--badge-color`, which a `Button` tone sets from `--button-<tone>-color`: the text colour, named `--<component>-text-color`, defaults to it, the background colour to it at the 20 step and the border colour at the 60 step, and a product may map any of the three in place of its default. The hover, active, pressed and disabled states of the `Button`, and the disabled state of the `Input`, derive from the rest value of each property at an alpha step unless the product maps that state; hover on the `Input` moves its border alone. A `Badge` rendered as a link, a button or another focusable element takes the `Button`'s focus ring and states. A `Button` pads its block by half its height less one line and its border (`--button-<size>-py`), with a line height of 1.25 (`--button-line-height`), so a wrapped label keeps off the border. The package now declares itself free of side effects apart from its CSS.
