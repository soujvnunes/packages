Add the `Bar`, `Field`, `ButtonGroup` and `Card` skeletons, with `CardTitle` and `CardDescription`, each with its stylesheet in `@soujvnunes/ui/styles.css` and its own subpath. The containers follow the control ladder: a bar, a field and a button group are 64 high with 24 of inline padding, the field and the button group take `--radius-40` and the bar takes no radius, and a card pads 12 on every side inside `--radius-24`. The `Card`, its `h2` title and its `p` description take their children as nodes or as a function that receives the wired props for the product's own element. A container publishes its inner radius, its radius less its inline padding, for the controls nested in it: `--ui-inner-radius-64` from a bar, a field or a button group, 16 by default from the field and the group and none from a bar with no radius, and `--ui-inner-radius-card` from a card, 12 by default. Each container is painted from one colour as the controls are, `--bar-color`, `--field-color`, `--button-group-color` or `--card-color`, which feeds its text, its background at the 20 step and its border at the 60 step unless the product maps one of them, and the field's invalid state reads its rest values until the product maps that state. A button group squares the inner sides of the buttons and inputs it joins, and a `Badge` takes `data-corner` to sit on a corner of its parent and share its radius there.
