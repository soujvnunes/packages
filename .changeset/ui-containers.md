Add the `Bar`, `Field`, `ButtonGroup` and `Card` skeletons, with `CardTitle` and `CardDescription`, each with its stylesheet in `@soujvnunes/ui/styles.css` and its own subpath. The containers follow the control ladder: a bar, a field and a button group are 64 high with 24 of inline padding, the field and the button group take `--radius-40` and the bar takes no radius, and a card pads 12 on every side inside `--radius-24`. The `Card`, its `h2` title and its `p` description take their children as nodes or as a function that receives the wired props for the product's own element. A container publishes its inner radius, its radius less its inline padding, for the controls nested in it: `--ui-inner-radius-64` from a bar, a field or a button group, 16 by default from the field and the group and none from a bar with no radius, and `--ui-inner-radius-card` from a card, 12 by default. A button group squares the inner sides of the buttons and inputs it joins, and a `Badge` takes `data-corner` to sit on a corner of its parent and share its radius there.
